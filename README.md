"WORLD IN A DAY" - a dark news-dashboard poster that sums up the world news
of Sunday 22 November 2026 in a single 1024x1024 image: the collapse of
the COP31 climate talks in Antalya after the EU and others rejected a pact
silent on fossil fuels, Iran's missile and drone strikes on US bases in
Bahrain, US intelligence chief Jay Clayton's public break with Trump over
Putin, Mexico's capture of cartel kingpin 'El Chapo Isidro', the Russian
missile strike on Kryvyi Rih, the landmark EU - Indonesia trade deal, the
Cikande candy-factory fire, India's 720 for 9 against New Zealand in
Wellington and the Seattle airport ICE arrests, plus a wire strip of
shorter items. The picture is drawn entirely by the program itself - a
small pure-stdlib stroke renderer with its own typeface and PNG writer -
so the output is deterministic, byte for byte.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
